# Structural Cardinal Numbers Proofs

All results below use only the axioms and rules of this model.

## Proposition 1: Form-Identical Structures Have One Profile

If two classified structures are structurally equal, they have structurally equal cardinal profiles.

### Proof

Let `A ≣ H`. By Rule 5:

`K(A) ≣ K(H)`

Therefore form-identical classified structures have one profile. QED.

## Proposition 2: A Recurrent Profile Is Not a Finite Profile

A recurrent loop is structurally distinct from a finite resolved structure.

### Proof

By Rule 1, a terminal structure receives `F`. By Rule 2, an unresolved single-route loop receives `R`. Axiom 6 states:

`F ≢ R`

Therefore the two recognized forms have distinct structural cardinal profiles. QED.

## Proposition 3: Parallel and Branching Forms Are Distinguished

A parallel recurrent form and a branching recurrent form have distinct profiles.

### Proof

By Rule 3, the parallel form receives `P`. By Rule 4, the branching form receives `B`. By Axiom 6:

`P ≢ B`

Therefore the model preserves their structural difference. QED.

## Proposition 4: Profile Difference Does Not Settle Classical Cardinality

A profile distinction alone does not prove a difference in Cantorian cardinal number.

### Proof

Axiom 7 and Rule 6 prohibit inference from profile distinction to a bijection claim or a classical cardinal claim. Therefore the models remain distinct until an additional set-theoretic interpretation is supplied. QED.
