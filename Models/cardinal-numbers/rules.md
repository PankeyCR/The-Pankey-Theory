# Structural Cardinal Numbers Rules

## Rule 1: Resolved Classification

A terminal structure collapses to its finite profile:

`A ▷ T ↓ Card(A) = F`

## Rule 2: Recurrent Classification

An unresolved single-route loop collapses to its recurrent profile:

`A ⟳ A : G ↓ Card(A) = R`

## Rule 3: Parallel Classification

A recurrent loop with parallel components collapses to its parallel profile:

`A ⟳ A : (G ∧ G) ↓ Card(A) = P`

## Rule 4: Branching Classification

A recurrent loop with alternatives collapses to its branching profile:

`A ⟳ A : (G ∨ G) ↓ Card(A) = B`

## Rule 5: Profile Preservation

Structurally equal classified forms preserve their cardinal profile:

`A ≣ H` and `Card(A) = K(A)` imply `K(A) ≣ K(H)`

## Rule 6: No Unfolding Inference

No rule may infer a classical cardinal, a completed totality, or a bijection solely from a recurrent or branching profile. Those claims require an additional set-theoretic model.
