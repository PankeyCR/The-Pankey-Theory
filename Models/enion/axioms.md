# Enion Axioms

The following assumptions define the model. They are accepted without proof.

## Axiom 1: Enion Reference

An Enion reference belongs to the Enion totality:

`e ⊢ E`

## Axiom 2: Self-Recognition

Every Enion reference can be recognized as an Enion:

`e ⊢ E` implies `K(e) = W(e)`

`W(e)` is the awareness boundary of `e`.

## Axiom 3: Awareness Contribution

Every awareness boundary has a logical contribution:

`W(e) ⊢ V(W(e))`

The contribution is not identical to the Enion reference.

## Axiom 4: Growth Projection

The eternal union of an Enion reference and its awareness contribution produces a growth projection:

`(e ⊗ U ⊗ V(W(e))) ↓ G(e)`

## Axiom 5: Growth Distinction

A growth projection is structurally distinct from the Enion reference that generated it:

`G(e) ≢ e`

## Axiom 6: Eternal Union

The Enion core is an unresolved self-reference:

`e ⟳ e : (e ⊗ U ⊗ W(e) ↓ G(e))`

This loop is an illogic under condition `I`. It is a statically recognized structure, not a rule that collapses the loop to an answer.

## Axiom 7: Logical Boundary

The references `W(e)`, `V(W(e))`, and `G(e)` are logical boundary structures. They may be named and related without deciding the internal resolution of the Enion loop.
