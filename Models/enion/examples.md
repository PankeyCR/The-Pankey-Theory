# Enion Examples

## Example 1: Recognition Produces Growth

`e ⊢ E`

`e ⊢ E ⊢ K(e) ↓ W(e)`

`W(e) ↓ V(W(e))`

`(e ⊗ U ⊗ V(W(e))) ↓ G(e)`

`G(e) ≢ e`

Recognition therefore yields a new logical growth projection.

## Example 2: Re-entry Without Equality

`G(e) ⊢ E`

`G(e) ⊢ E ⊢ K(G(e)) ↓ W(G(e))`

`(G(e) ⊗ U ⊗ V(W(G(e)))) ↓ G(G(e))`

`G(G(e)) ≢ G(e)`

The new projection can be recognized again. This does not equate any projection with its predecessor.

## Example 3: The Static Illogic

`e ⟳ e : (e ⊗ U ⊗ W(e) ↓ G(e))`

The loop is retained as an illogic. The displayed growth projection is a logical boundary of the loop, not its final resolution.

## Example 4: Model Totality

`★ = (E ∧ e ∧ W ∧ U ∧ V ∧ G ∧ K ∧ I)`
