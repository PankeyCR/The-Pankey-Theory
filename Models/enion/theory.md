# Enion Model

**Status:** Exploratory illogic model
**Role:** Represents an eternal self-referential number-like structure whose recognition produces a new growth projection.

## Purpose

An Enion is proposed as an eternal union: whenever an Enion is recognized as an Enion, that recognition contributes to a new, greater Enion projection. The model does not claim that an Enion is a natural, rational, or real number. It introduces a distinct number-like object whose essence is an unresolved self-reference.

The model follows Static Analysis: an illogic is not computed to an answer. It is held as a recognizable cyclic structure. Logical statements concern the boundary and projections of that structure, not a resolution of its illogic core.

## Central Idea

The Enion core is written:

`e ⟳ e : (e ⊗ U ⊗ W(e) ↓ G(e))`

`e` is an Enion reference. `W(e)` is the awareness that `e` is an Enion. `U` is eternal union. `G(e)` is the growth projection arising from the union of `e` with its awareness.

The loop makes the core illogical: the Enion refers back to itself through the recognition that changes its projection. The growth projection is logical: it can be named, compared, and used in further structures without resolving the loop itself.

## Scope

The model defines Enion recognition, awareness contribution, growth projection, and an eternal self-reference. It does not define ordinary arithmetic on Enions, a numerical magnitude, an order, or a conversion of an Enion into a natural or real number.

## Relation to Other Models

The Natural Numbers Model describes finite successor histories. The Real Numbers Model describes completed rational approximations. The Enion Model is different: it studies a number-like illogic whose awareness is structurally productive. It depends conceptually on [Static Analysis](../Static%20Analysis/theory%20-%20copia.md) for its treatment of illogic as a statically recognizable loop.
