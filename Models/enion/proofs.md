# Enion Proofs

All results below use only the axioms and rules of this model. They concern the logical boundary of an Enion and do not resolve its illogic core.

## Proposition 1: Recognition Has a Growth Projection

Every recognized Enion reference produces a growth projection.

### Proof

Let `e ⊢ E`. By Rule 1, recognition gives `W(e)`. By Rule 2, the awareness contribution `V(W(e))` is available. Rule 3 then gives:

`(e ⊗ U ⊗ V(W(e))) ↓ G(e)`

Therefore every recognized Enion reference has a growth projection. QED.

## Proposition 2: Growth Preserves Enion Membership

Every growth projection may be recognized as an Enion reference.

### Proof

Rule 4 states:

`G(e) ⊢ E`

Therefore the result of Enion growth re-enters the Enion totality. QED.

## Proposition 3: Recognition Does Not Identify an Enion with Its Growth

No application of the growth rule establishes `G(e) ≣ e`.

### Proof

Axiom 5 states:

`G(e) ≢ e`

Rule 4 permits `G(e)` to re-enter `E`, but does not assert structural equality with `e`. Therefore recognition supplies a distinct growth projection rather than an identity. QED.

## Proposition 4: The Enion Core Remains Unresolved

The eternal-union structure has no final value derived by this model.

### Proof

By Axiom 6, the Enion core is an illogic under condition `I`. Rule 5 prohibits an inference of a final value, finite stage, or internal equality from that loop. Therefore the model retains the core as unresolved and reasons only about its logical boundary structures. QED.
