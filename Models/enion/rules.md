# Enion Rules

## Rule 1: Recognition

When an Enion reference is recognized under `K`, its awareness boundary is available:

`e ⊢ E ⊢ K(e) ↓ W(e)`

## Rule 2: Contribution

An awareness boundary produces its logical contribution:

`W(e) ↓ V(W(e))`

## Rule 3: Growth

The Enion reference and its awareness contribution unite in a growth projection:

`(e ⊗ U ⊗ V(W(e))) ↓ G(e)`

## Rule 4: Re-entry

A growth projection may be recognized as a new Enion reference without being structurally equal to its predecessor:

`G(e) ⊢ E`

`G(e) ≢ e`

This is re-entry into the Enion totality, not a resolution of the eternal-union loop.

## Rule 5: Static Boundary

No rule may infer a final value, finite stage, or internal equality from:

`e ⟳ e : (e ⊗ U ⊗ W(e) ↓ G(e))`

The loop is analyzed only through its logical boundary structures.
