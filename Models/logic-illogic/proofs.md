# Logic and Illogic Proofs

All results below use only the axioms and rules of this model.

## Proposition 1: Direct Forms Are Classified by Their Operator

`W ▷ T` is logical and `A ⟳ A` is illogical.

### Proof

By Rule 1, `▷` classifies the first direct form as logical and `⟳` classifies the second direct form as illogical. This is the distinction required by Axiom 2. QED.

## Proposition 2: A Via Structure Preserves Classification

Adding a via structure does not change a logical form into an illogical form, or an illogical form into a logical form.

### Proof

Rule 2 extends `W ▷ T` to `W ▷ T : Q` and `A ⟳ A` to `A ⟳ A : B`. In each extension, the classification operator remains unchanged. Therefore the classification is preserved. QED.

## Proposition 3: A Condition Completes a Classification

A condition is part of a complete form without changing its classification.

### Proof

By Rule 3, a via form receives a condition to become `W ▷ T : Q ⊢ R` or `A ⟳ A : B ⊢ C`. By Rule 4, the condition may be interpreted, but the classification operator is unchanged. Therefore the condition completes rather than replaces the classification. QED.

## Proposition 4: Compound Positions Preserve Classification

Multiple references in one position do not change a logical form into an illogical form, or an illogical form into a logical form.

### Proof

By Axiom 5 and Rule 5, references may be joined by `∧` or `∨` in the origin, outcome, via, or condition position. The classification operator remains `▷` or `⟳`. Therefore the classification is preserved. QED.

## Proposition 5: A Hybrid Retains Both Classifications

A hybrid does not reduce its components to one classification.

### Proof

Rule 6 permits a logical and an illogical complete form to join through `∧` or `∨`. The rule requires the hybrid to retain the classification of both components. Therefore the hybrid contains both classifications. QED.
