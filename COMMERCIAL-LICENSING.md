# Commercial Licensing Requests

Commercial use of material governed by the repository's CC BY-NC 4.0 license, and all use of the proprietary Resolution Desk website and operating materials beyond personal non-commercial evaluation, requires prior written permission.

To request a commercial license, contact the repository owner through the GitHub repository and include:

- your organization and contact details;
- the material you want to use;
- your intended product, service, or internal use;
- whether you need modification, redistribution, hosting, sublicensing, or white-label rights;
- the countries or territories involved; and
- the proposed duration and scale of use.

No commercial permission is granted until the copyright holder and requester sign a written agreement. The agreement should define scope, fees, attribution, confidentiality, support, liability, termination, and governing law.

This document is an invitation to discuss commercial licensing, not an offer or a binding license.
