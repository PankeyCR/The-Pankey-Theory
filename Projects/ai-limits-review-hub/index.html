Review protocol

Three outcomes, kept separate.

01

Bounded response

A system returned a finite answer under stated conditions. It may still be uncertain, incomplete, or assumption-dependent.

02

Validated resolution

A response has a stated, independently checkable method that supports the answer for the declared question and conditions.

03

Unresolved structure

No terminal result is declared, or the available information cannot support one. A responsible response should identify the boundary.

Founding pilot

Turn AI outputs into reviewable records.

A fixed-scope pilot audits a selected batch of AI answers, identifies assumptions and unsupported conclusions, then delivers a shareable findings report.

  • 20 to 50 AI outputs from one real workflow
  • Structured findings for evidence, assumptions, and validation
  • Before-and-after comparison for corrected prompts or responses
