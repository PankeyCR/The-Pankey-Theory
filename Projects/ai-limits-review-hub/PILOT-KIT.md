# Pilot Kit

This kit turns Resolution Desk into a small, testable service. It is not legal, tax, privacy, or regulatory advice.

## Initial Offer

**Offer:** Audit 20 to 50 AI outputs from one workflow and deliver a structured report that separates supported claims, assumptions, missing conditions, and validation methods.

**Do not promise:** guaranteed truth, compliance certification, legal/medical/financial advice, consciousness correction, or overcoming fundamental AI limits.

**Suggested first pilot:** Fixed scope, one workflow, one to two weeks, a clear written deliverable. Set the price after understanding the material, time required, and value at risk. A small paid pilot is better than a large unpaid custom engagement.

## Discovery Questions

Use these questions in an initial conversation:

1. Which AI workflow creates the most expensive rework or uncertainty?
2. What decision is someone making from the AI output?
3. What evidence currently supports or checks that output?
4. What happens when the AI is wrong or overconfident?
5. Can you supply 20 to 50 representative, non-sensitive examples?
6. What would a useful improvement look like after two weeks?

## Review Record

For each answer, record:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Question | The original task given to the AI. |
| Conditions | Facts, constraints, data sources, and definitions supplied. |
| Response | The exact AI output reviewed. |
| Assumptions | Facts or meanings introduced but not supplied. |
| Evidence | Sources, calculations, or rules used. |
| Validation method | A check that can independently support the conclusion. |
| Classification | Bounded response; validated limited resolution; or unresolved structure. |
| Correction | A revised prompt, response, or escalation step. |

## Success Metrics

Agree on metrics before starting. Use at least two:

- number of unsupported claims found;
- number of hidden assumptions made explicit;
- percentage of outputs with a stated validation method;
- reduction in human rework after revised prompts;
- number of cases correctly escalated as unresolved instead of treated as certain.

## Before Handling Client Data

Confirm in writing:

- what data may be shared and what must be removed or anonymized;
- who owns the inputs, review records, and final report;
- the service's limits and exclusions;
- how long you keep material and how it will be deleted;
- the fee, payment terms, delivery date, and revision scope.

## Finding Prospective Users

Begin with people who already use AI for internal summaries, research, proposals, customer support, or operations. Share one example audit and ask about a specific workflow where AI errors cost time, money, or trust. Offer the fixed-scope pilot only after you understand their use case.

## Deliverable Outline

1. Executive summary: workflow, scope, and limits.
2. Findings: recurring assumptions, evidence gaps, and unsupported claims.
3. Case examples: original output, analysis, and revision.
4. Metrics: agreed measures before and after review.
5. Recommendations: prompt changes, validation steps, and escalation criteria.
